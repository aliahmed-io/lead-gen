# Operating plan

1. Preserve originals and keep generated/unsourced contacts quarantined in the working database.
2. Set your identity, address, portfolio and offer. Validate templates using their final preview.
3. Rotate exposed legacy credentials; configure mailboxes, provider DNS and HTTPS hosting.
4. Review provenance and relevance, externally verify a small segment and enroll it deliberately.
5. Test delivery with your own recipients, authentication, replies, opt-out and backup restoration.
6. Start a small pilot, monitor replies/bounces and expand based on observed qualified conversations.

The dashboard is the control surface; the explicit worker is the background process. Runtime modules share SQLite, delivery, verification, templates and account rules. Provider setup and actual lead research remain operator responsibilities. See README.md and REPAIR-STATUS.md.
